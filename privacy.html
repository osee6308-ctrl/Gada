Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gada ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Content you post — text and images you upload
  • Interactions — likes, comments, and shares you make
  • Device information — browser type, operating system, screen size
  • Usage data — how you use the app, time spent, features accessed

2. Where Data Is Stored

All data you create inside Gada is stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage). We do not upload your posts, images, or personal data to any external server unless you explicitly share it.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your posts and interactions in the app
  • To improve app features and fix bugs
  • To enforce our Terms of Service

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties, except:

  • When required by law
  • To protect the safety of users
  • With your explicit consent

5. Children's Privacy

Gada is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

  • Delete your content at any time by clearing the app's storage
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Contact us for any privacy concerns

7. Cookies and Tracking

Gada does not use cookies or third-party tracking scripts.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
